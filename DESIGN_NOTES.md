# Bharatnomy — Concept B: Civic Signals

## Visual direction
A light, contemporary data-editorial identity distinct from Concept A's botanical/sage palette. The revised system uses warm chalk, cobalt blue, pale periwinkle, vermilion and muted golden yellow. The interface stays light; deep blue is reserved for typography, controls and small high-contrast accents, not full-page dark backgrounds.

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces (editorial, distinctive, high contrast)
- UI/body: Space Grotesk (precise, contemporary)
- Metadata/data labels: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout language
- Geometric split hero with cartographic grid and a raised signal graphic
- Square-edged brand mark and compact technical header
- Large editorial chapter headlines and numbered navigation rail
- Five research lenses presented as a connected systems diagram and ledger
- Warm field-note panel for Maharashtra heat research
- Pale-blue observatory surface with cobalt controls, coral selection and bright table/chart views
- Subpages share the same civic/cartographic visual grammar

## Data integrity
Both included datasets are synthetic interface demonstrations, not official statistics. The district visualization is schematic and does not represent authoritative district boundaries.
